📖 Week 3 Framework
The Re-entry Ritual
A structured guide for showing up socially after a long pause — without pressure, performance, or pretending.
🛡️ AI-generated with therapist-reviewed methodology · Not a substitute for therapy
Reflection prompts
Prompt 01
What does "showing up" mean to you right now — and how is that different from what it meant two years ago?
Take 5 minutes. Write freely. Don't edit.
Prompt 02
Name one small moment from this week where you felt genuinely yourself — even briefly.
It doesn't have to be social. A walk counts.
Prompt 03
What's the specific story you tell yourself before you decline an invitation?
Naming the story is the first step to changing it.
Reframe exercises
Reframe 01 — The Expectation Trap
"I need to be interesting / funny / 'on' when I go out."
↓
"My only job is to show up. The rest is optional."
Reframe 02 — The Energy Myth
"I'll go when I feel ready / have more energy."
↓
"Energy follows action, not the other way around. Small steps first."
This week's actions
1
Attend one event from your prescription. Not two. One.
2
Say your name first to one person you haven't met. That's the whole goal.
3
Journal for 5 minutes after the event. Not what happened — how you felt walking in.
A note from the methodology
Research on social re-entry consistently shows the threshold moment is walking through the door — not how the event goes. Your nervous system needs repetition, not perfection.
