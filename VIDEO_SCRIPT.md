# Reroot — Demo Video Script

**Target length:** 90 seconds
**Tone:** calm, product-focused, no hype
**Narration:** voiceover (your voice) over screen recording

---

## Recording checklist before you start

- [ ] Open the app on Railway (reroot.up.railway.app) in Chrome
- [ ] Set browser zoom to 110% and window to ~390px wide (mobile size) — or use Chrome DevTools mobile view (iPhone 14 Pro)
- [ ] Clear app state: Profile → Reset → start fresh
- [ ] Use your real Google account for sign-in
- [ ] Have OBS or Windows Game Bar (Win + G) ready to record
- [ ] Record at 1080p, 30fps minimum

## Scene-by-scene

---

### SCENE 1 — The problem (0:00–0:12)

**Screen:** nothing. Black or a simple slide.

**Voiceover:**
> "Social isolation doesn't announce itself. After a breakup, a move, a burnout — suddenly getting out feels like a lot. Most apps don't help. Event lists are overwhelming. Social apps feel high-stakes. There's nothing in between."

**Cut to:** app opening on the onboarding screen.

---

### SCENE 2 — Onboarding (0:12–0:25)

**Screen:** Onboarding screen → Google Sign-In → name pre-filled → tap "Entrar no Cohort"

**Voiceover:**
> "Reroot is a 12-week guide for social re-entry. You join a cohort — a small group of people at the same stage. Not thousands of strangers."

**What to show:**
- Tap Google Sign-In → name auto-fills
- Scroll through interests briefly
- Tap "Entrar no Cohort Primavera"

---

### SCENE 3 — Identity Mirror (0:25–0:40)

**Screen:** IdentityMirror — select situation → select goal → 2 more steps → affirmation screen

**Voiceover:**
> "The app starts by understanding *why* you're isolated — not just what you like. Are you recovering from a breakup? New to the city? Burned out? Your answers shape everything that follows."

**What to show:**
- Tap "Trabalhando remoto, perdendo contato" (or whichever fits)
- Tap "Fazer novos amigos"
- Move through steps quickly (2s each)
- Pause 1s on the affirmation screen — let the copy land

---

### SCENE 4 — Diagnostic (0:40–0:50)

**Screen:** Diagnostic screen

**Voiceover:**
> "The diagnostic shows your profile and prescription — calibrated to where you are, not where you should be."

**What to show:**
- Scroll slowly through the diagnostic
- Pause on the prescription lines (the 3 rx bullet points)

---

### SCENE 5 — Home screen (0:50–1:05)

**Screen:** Home screen → scroll through → tap an event card

**Voiceover:**
> "The home screen gives you a prescription: one or two events for this week, chosen for your situation. Not a feed. A recommendation."

**What to show:**
- Show the greeting + prescription pad
- Show 1–2 event cards with cohort members
- Tap RSVP on one event → show the toast notification "Lembrete salvo"
- Scroll to "Ideias para você" briefly

---

### SCENE 6 — Venues from Google Places (1:05–1:20)

**Screen:** Events tab → tap "Bares & Cafés" filter → show venue list → tap one → detail panel

**Voiceover:**
> "The Bars & Cafés section pulls real data from Google Places — actual venues in Curitiba, with ratings, prices, and a reason why this specific place works for where you are right now."

**What to show:**
- Switch to Events tab
- Tap "🍺 Bares & Cafés" chip
- Show the compact venue list (several rows visible at once)
- Tap "☕ Cafés" sub-filter — list updates
- Tap a café → detail panel opens
- Show the rating block (⭐ + number of reviews)
- Show the green "Por que é bom pra você agora" card
- Tap "Ver no Google Maps" link

---

**Screen:** Back to home screen. Still.

**Voiceover:**

## Editing notes

- **Music:** lo-fi, instrumental, quiet. Fits the calm/therapeutic tone. Look for "lofi chill" on YouTube Audio Library (free).
- **Transitions:** simple cuts only. No wipes or zooms — they make portfolio videos look amateur.
- **Captions:** optional but good for LinkedIn autoplay (muted). Use CapCut auto-captions or add manually.
- **Thumbnail:** a clean screenshot of the home screen prescription pad, with "Reroot" as overlay text.
